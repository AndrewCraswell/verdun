# Commercially Available WW1 Soldier Character Assets (Realistic, Semi-Realistic, Synty Comparison)

All prices and statuses below were observed on 2026-10-03 (USD unless noted). Fab prices are given as "Personal / Professional" license tiers where they differ. Fab technical data (triangle counts, texture resolution, engine versions) was read from Fab's own listing data for each product, so it reflects what the seller entered or what Fab's file analyzer computed.

## 1. Which French WW1 (Poilu, horizon blue, Adrian helmet, 1915-1916) soldier models exist?

### Takeaway
Only a few commercial French WW1 soldiers exist. No listing found is all of: realistic, game-ready (<30k tris), rigged and LOD'd. The realistic ones are cinematic or high-poly: Albin's rigged Poilu (79k polys, UDIM), DarkTide's MetaHuman "1916" outfit kit, and Pixe4Fiction's Character Creator outfit. The crowd-friendly French options are all low-poly or semi-stylized: AmeliaTeale's Entente pack (about 3.9k verts) and Batuhan Ceyhan's hand-painted pack.

### Inferences
- For a realistic 1916 Poilu, Albin's model is the most complete off-the-shelf mesh: coat, backpack, Adrian helmet, Mixamo-demoed rig.
  - It is roughly 3x over a crowd budget.
  - Its UDIM layout would need rebaking to a single 0-1 UV atlas for glTF/Babylon, because glTF has no native UDIM support. This last point is general knowledge, not from the listing.
- The only French WW1 options already within a hundreds-on-screen budget are the AmeliaTeale and Batuhan Ceyhan packs. Both are explicitly low-poly or stylized. AmeliaTeale's is closer to "realistic-stylized": human proportions, 1k textures, swappable heads and facial hair.
- The DarkTide and Reallusion routes both need a host character system (MetaHuman or CC4) plus a decimation/export step before they could reach Babylon.

### Gaps
- No realistic, rigged, LOD'd, sub-30k-tri French Poilu was found on Fab, CGTrader, TurboSquid, Unity Asset Store, RenderHub or Sketchfab.
- The era of Raist_lin's TurboSquid "World War French Soldier" (WW1 vs WW2) could not be verified from the listing text.
- Whether Pixe4Fiction ever released the Adrian helmet (or its promised U.S. variants) as a separate Reallusion item was not verified.

## 2. Which German WW1 models exist (feldgrau, Stahlhelm M1916 or Pickelhaube)?

German WW1 options include:
- AmeliaTeale's low-poly German pack (3.6k verts, 3 helmets, 20 animations)
- Studio FieriaGold's "Soldiers WW1" (5 German and 4 British soldiers, 3-9k verts, Epic skeleton)
- MoonyState's high-poly Pickelhaube uniform (unrigged)
- DarkTide's early-war 1914 MetaHuman kit
- several old TurboSquid models (17k-30k polys)

Stahlhelm M1916 helmets are available as separate kitbash meshes. Realistic WW2 Germans (TalkingDrums, wellweldedgames) are the best-engineered game characters to kitbash from.

## 4. Rigging: humanoid / Epic / Mixamo compatibility

### Takeaway
Most game-oriented WW1 packs are skinned to the **UE4/Epic mannequin skeleton**: AmeliaTeale, FieriaGold, Batuhan Ceyhan, Alexey-gruzovik, TalkingDrums. The DarkTide MetaHuman kits follow the UE mannequin skeleton. Albin's French model uses a custom Blender rig that the seller demonstrated with Mixamo mocap. The high-poly MoonyState models are unrigged.

### Cited Findings
- AmeliaTeale packs: "Characters skinned to the UE4 skeleton… Easy to re-target animations (All the poses are taken from the animation starter pack)".
  - Extra metacarpal joints (index/middle/ring/pinky_00) and an extra forearm twist joint (lowerarm_twist_02).

## 9. Character Creator 4 / Reallusion route (WW1 uniforms, LOD export, licensing)

### Takeaway
Reallusion has one confirmed WW1 product: Pixe4Fiction's **Great War French Soldier Outfit** (Poilu 1916-18; Adrian helmet sold separately). No German WW1 CC content was found. The CC4 game pipeline can produce game-ready characters: a 10K-poly Game Character Base, InstaLOD reduction and remeshing, LOD0-6, texture baking and material merging, and FBX export for UE and Unity, with multiple LODs in one FBX.

**Licensing is the catch for crowds.** A Standard content license "only grants outputs for one single character" in commercial games. Mass character output needs the separately purchased **Extended License**.

### Cited Findings
- Great War French Soldier Outfit (Pixe4Fiction): $29.50 list ($20.65 on sale), CC v4.10+, released 2022-12-16, 1916-1918 uniform, Adrian helmet sold separately. — [Reallusion Content Store](https://www.reallusion.com/contentstore/pack/character-creator/3d-cloth/great-war-french-soldier-outfit)
- Other Reallusion military content found is WW2 or modern:
  - "WW2 British Uniform Pack" — [Reallusion](https://www.reallusion.com/contentstore/pack/character-creator/3d-cloth/ww2-british-uniform-pack)
  - "WW2 German Uniform" — [Reallusion](https://www.reallusion.com/ContentStore/Pack/ww2-german-uniform)
  - WWII German bonnet/helmet and pants/boots packs — [Reallusion Marketplace](https://marketplace.reallusion.com/wwii-german-soldier-bonnet-and-helmet-pack)
  - "Napoleonic Soldier" — [Reallusion Marketplace](https://marketplace.reallusion.com/napoleon-soldier)
- CC game pipeline features:
  - "10K polygon base" Game Character Base, for "massive amount of NPCs".
  - A sample with 7 LODs (LOD0-6): facial animation kept in LOD0-2; LOD4-6 generated by the Remesher as single mesh, single material.
  - InstaLOD polygon reduction by target count or percentage; Remesher merges meshes.
  - Texture baking (512-4K); material merging to cut draw calls (up to 4K). FBX for the UE4 rig and Unity Mecanim. — [Reallusion Game Pipeline](https://www.reallusion.com/character-creator/game-pipeline.html)
- "In Character Creator 3.1 and later, the LOD characters are included in one FBX file along with JSON files". — [CC4 manual: Exporting Character with LOD](https://manual.reallusion.com/Character-Creator-4/Content/ENU/4.0/17_Export/Exported_Files_from_Different_Versions.htm)
- Content licensing:
  - Standard License permits export to third-party software including Unreal and Unity, in "fbx, obj, usd" and other formats.
  - "one Standard License only grants outputs for one single character" in commercial games and XR.
  - "Extended License grants mass character outputs with no limitation for use in commercial games, XR projects and online interactive services", and must be bought separately.
  - Clients "aren't allowed to republish licensed content for sale in online stores". — [Reallusion Content License](https://www.reallusion.com/license/content.html)
- Older CC documentation framed game-engine use as requiring an "export license" version of content. — [Reallusion export page (search summary)](https://www.reallusion.com/character-creator/export-3d-character.html)

### Inferences
- A CC4 Poilu crowd built from Pixe4Fiction's outfit would likely need the outfit's **Extended License**, plus Extended Licenses for any other CC components (heads, hair, helmet), to deploy many variants. Budget accordingly.
- CC4 exports FBX, not glTF (glTF is not mentioned on the pipeline page), so an FBX-to-glTF step would be needed for Babylon.
- CC4's InstaLOD tooling (LODs, material merge, texture bake) is the most complete built-in path found for turning a realistic uniform into crowd-ready LOD chains.

### Gaps
- Extended License prices for the Pixe4Fiction outfit were not visible.
- Native CC4 glTF/GLB export was not confirmed.
- No German WW1 uniform or Stahlhelm M16 / Pickelhaube was found in the Reallusion stores. The Reallusion marketplace search page did not render via automated browsing, so smaller third-party items may have been missed.
- Whether exported characters must be protected or encrypted in shipped games is not addressed in the license page text retrieved.

## 10. Marketplace status, WW1-game asset makers, Daz3D, and licensing for cross-engine use

### Takeaway
Fab is now the main storefront:
- The **Sketchfab Store has closed** and its paid models were migrated to Fab (e.g., MoonyState's WW1 models).
- UE Marketplace URLs redirect to Fab.
- Fab's Standard License allows use in any engine, which covers exporting UE-format WW1 packs for Babylon.
- MetaHumans may now be used in other engines.
- No assets from shipped WW1 games were found for sale, and Daz3D has no WW1 uniform content.

# Verdun, 1916: the weeks of the battle

The dated events behind the loading screen's line of history (S6 in [interface](../../../design/interface.md#s6-loading),
`apps/client/src/ui/front/verdun-1916.ts`). One line per turning point, in the order they happened. The game shows the
latest event of the week being fought, for up to three weeks after it.

Written from the standard histories, not yet checked line by line against them: Alistair Horne, _The Price of Glory:
Verdun 1916_ (1962); Paul Jankowski, _Verdun: The Longest Battle of the Great War_ (2013); and the Mémorial de Verdun's
chronology. Dates marked _check_ are the least certain.

| Date                | Event                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| January–February    | The salient quiet; German guns, shells and divisions gathered in the woods north of Verdun (Operation _Gericht_)               |
| 12 February         | The offensive's first date; postponed for snow and fog, giving the French days to bring up reinforcements                      |
| 21 February         | The bombardment opens at dawn, over 1,200 guns on a front of a few kilometres; the infantry goes forward in the afternoon      |
| 22 February         | Lt-Col. Émile Driant killed as the Bois des Caures falls                                                                       |
| 25 February         | Fort Douaumont, disarmed and nearly empty, taken by a few men of the 24th Brandenburg Regiment; Pétain given command at Verdun |
| From late February  | The Bar-le-Duc–Verdun road (named the Voie Sacrée later, by Barrès) carries the battle's supplies and men by lorry             |
| 6 March             | The attack extended to the left bank: Le Mort-Homme and Hill 304                                                               |
| 10 April            | Pétain's order of the day ends "Courage ! On les aura !" (_check_: dated 9 or 10 April in different accounts)                  |
| 1 May               | Nivelle succeeds Pétain at the head of the Second Army; Pétain promoted to command the Army Group Centre                       |
| Early May           | Hill 304 taken by the Germans (_check_ the date)                                                                               |
| 22–24 May           | Mangin's division breaks into Fort Douaumont and is driven out                                                                 |
| Late May            | Le Mort-Homme's crest taken by the Germans (_check_: around 29 May)                                                            |
| 1–7 June            | Fort Vaux besieged; Major Raynal's garrison fights in the galleries                                                            |
| 7 June              | Fort Vaux surrenders, its garrison without water                                                                               |
| 23 June             | Attack behind a barrage of phosgene (Green Cross) shells; Thiaumont and Fleury taken, the slopes of Souville reached           |
| 1 July              | The Somme offensive opens, drawing German guns and divisions away                                                              |
| 11–12 July          | The last German attack on Fort Souville fails; the Germans go over to the defensive at Verdun                                  |
| 18 August           | The Régiment d'infanterie coloniale du Maroc retakes the ruins of Fleury (_check_: 17–18 August)                               |
| 29 August           | Hindenburg replaces Falkenhayn as Chief of the General Staff; the offensive is called off in early September                   |
| 24 October          | Fort Douaumont retaken in fog, after a bombardment including 400 mm railway guns                                               |
| 2 November          | The Germans evacuate Fort Vaux and the French reoccupy it                                                                      |
| 15–18 December      | A last French attack retakes Louvemont and Bezonvaux; the battle ends                                                          |
